## About

Junior Software Support Specialist at **EvTrack (Pty) Ltd**, Cape Town. I work on visitor management and access control systems, helping clients get the most out of the platform and the hardware behind it.

Alongside that, I'm a computer engineer by training, with a background spanning embedded systems and AI-enabled software. Bilingual: English and French.

## What I do

| Area | Details |
|---|---|
| Support | First line technical support for EvTrack's visitor management and access control platform (web portal, kiosks, guard app, visitor app, hardware readers) |
| Training | Client-facing training and onboarding, including data imports and system configuration |
| Coordination | Bridge between clients, the development team, and the wider support team on technical issues |
